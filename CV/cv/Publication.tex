I am a second-year MPhil student majoring in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. \href{https://citymind.top/}{\textbf{Yuxuan Liang}}. My research focuses on Multi-modal Learning/Spatio-temporal Data Mining/Time Series Forecasting, and currently includes \textbf{6 CCF-A}, \textbf{2 CCF-B} publications, with other first-author submissions under review, 100+ citations.
